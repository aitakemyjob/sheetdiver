# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SheetDiver to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and constructive — especially with newcomers
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback on code and ideas
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Trolling and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and anywhere you officially represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers by contacting
[@aitakemyjob](https://github.com/aitakemyjob) on GitHub. Reports are
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful. Responses scale
with severity: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a
permanent ban.
